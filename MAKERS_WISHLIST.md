# Makers wishlist

Candidates to add to the browser. Stats from the YouTube Data API, November 2026.
"Fit" is a rough call on how much of the channel is inventions or builds (vs science, reviews, comedy).

**Status (Nov 2026):** every maker on this list is now on the site (46 in total). This file is kept as a record of where they came from.

## Suggested order

1. **Quick wins:** small catalogs where nearly every video is a build. Stuff Made Here, Michael Reeves.
2. **Core of the crowd:** Colin Furze, Laura Kampf, Allen Pan, James Bruton, Xyla Foxlin, William Osman, The Backyard Scientist.
3. **Bigger catalogs:** Wintergatan, Integza, Hacksmith Industries, Mark Rober, I Did A Thing.
4. **Adam Savage (Tested):** 7,859 uploads, mostly not Adam building. Filter to One Day Builds and his own projects.

## Found through maker events

Speakers and exhibitors from Open Sauce, Hackaday Supercon / Hackaday Europe, EMF Camp and the Chaos Communication Congress (36C3–38C3),
matched to YouTube channels and checked by hand: real maker or engineering content, not a company, not a namesake. Subscriber counts as of
November 2026. "Last" is the latest upload. MakeFest and Toronto Maker Festival are not covered yet (their exhibitor lists are names only).

How they were found: Open Sauce's site links its featured creators' channels and lists every exhibitor with a bio; Hackaday's talks are on its
YouTube channel with speaker names in the titles; EMF Camp publishes its schedule as data; media.ccc.de lists each Congress talk's speakers.
Names were turned into channels by trying @handles built from the name, then filtering out strangers who share the name.

## Out of scope

The site is for makers who make things. These channels were added, then removed after review because they mainly review, repair, restore, assemble kits or comment:

- **Kynosys**: Tool reviews; nothing of his own made on the channel.
- **Ken Shirriff**: Reverse-engineers and restores other people's chips and computers.
- **Chris Gammell**: Talks and tutorials; almost nothing built on camera.
- **Uncle Jessy**: Printer reviews, deals and prints of other people's models.
- **Canuck Creator**: Assembles commercial printer kits; firmware guides and reviews.
- **3D Printing Nerd**: Reviews and showcase prints of other people's designs.
