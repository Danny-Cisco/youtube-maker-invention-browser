# Makers wishlist

Candidates to add to the browser. Stats from the YouTube Data API, November 2026.
"Fit" is a rough call on how much of the channel is inventions or builds (vs science, reviews, comedy).

**Status (Nov 2026):** every maker on this list is now on the site (46 in total). This file is kept as a record of where they came from.

## Suggested order

1. **Quick wins:** small catalogs where nearly every video is a build. Stuff Made Here, Michael Reeves.
2. **Core of the crowd:** Colin Furze, Laura Kampf, Allen Pan, James Bruton, Xyla Foxlin, William Osman, The Backyard Scientist.
3. **Bigger catalogs:** Wintergatan, Integza, Hacksmith Industries, Mark Rober, I Did A Thing.
4. **Adam Savage (Tested):** 7,859 uploads, mostly not Adam building. Filter to One Day Builds and his own projects.
